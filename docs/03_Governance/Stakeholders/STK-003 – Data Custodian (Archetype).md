## Responsibilities
- Define governance needs for dataset sharing (intent, approvals, revocation).
- Provide the “data owner” decision point for human-in-the-loop request approval (simulated).

## Expectations
- Strong identity assurance, explicit approvals, and auditable access decisions.
- Ability to revoke access and respond to suspected compromise.
