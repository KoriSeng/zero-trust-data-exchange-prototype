## Assumption Statement
Approximately 15–20 hours per week will be available to execute the planned 12-week phased project and associated documentation.

## Rationale
The proposal includes a time management note and a phased plan relying on sustained weekly effort.

## Impact if False
Reduced available time increases the likelihood of scope reduction, schedule slip, or incomplete evaluation evidence.

## Validation Plan
Track weekly time spent and compare against plan; if variance persists, record a scope/schedule decision via a new Decision Record.
